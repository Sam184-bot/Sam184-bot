About Me

Designing with texture, story, and material.

I am a Textile Design student focused on translating concepts into tactile textile outcomes through mixed media, loom-based weaving, and print development. My practice is deeply rooted in material-led exploration and an interest in how handcrafted surfaces carry expression, memory, and culture.

I enjoy navigating between experimentation and structure—combining textile techniques with visual storytelling to create meaningful, sensory forms that feel both contemporary and rooted in process.
